3. The problem

The menu page

The menu is a scanned PDF from 2023. It takes 4.1 seconds to open on 4G, the text is too small to read without zooming, and three prices are out of date. There is no order button on it.

Checkout on a phone

Ordering a flat white takes seven taps and two page loads. For a small menu, three is normal. Payment only accepts cards typed by hand.

💡 The rule for next quarter: Every tap between “I want a coffee” and “paid” costs you orders.

4. The plan

  1. Replace the PDF menu with a real menu page (week 1). Searchable, readable on a phone, with an order button on every item.
  2. Cut checkout to three taps (week 2). Add Apple Pay and Google Pay.
  3. Load in under 2 seconds (week 2). Compress the photos and remove the unused booking plugin.
  4. Add a 10% direct-order code (week 3). Printed on delivery-app bags.
  5. Measure and report (week 6). Compared with this report.
