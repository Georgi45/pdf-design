Studio Example Q3 summary
01 Summary

Orders grew. The website did not.

People want your coffee. They are buying it in spite of the menu page, not because of it.

+38 % online orders against Q2, almost all of it from Instagram and Google Maps.
4.1 s to load the menu on a phone. People expect it in under two and a half.
62 % of phone visitors leave on the menu page without ordering anything.
What is holding you back

The menu weighs 4.1 MB. On a phone it crawls, and two out of three people give up before they reach the order button.

What is working

Instagram brings 41 % of new customers, and those customers come back 3.2 times a month.
