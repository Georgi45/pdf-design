03 · The problem

Two problems, one cause

The menu page

A 9 MB PDF nobody can use on a phone

The menu is a scanned PDF from 2023. It takes 4.1 seconds to open on 4G, the text is too small to read without zooming, and three prices are out of date.

There is no order button on it. A customer who has decided has to go back, find the order page and search again.

Checkout on a phone

Seven taps between hungry and paid

Ordering a flat white takes seven taps and two page loads. For a small menu, three is normal.

Payment only accepts cards typed by hand. Apple Pay and Google Pay would remove two of the seven taps.

The rule for next quarter

Every tap between “I want a coffee” and “paid” costs you orders.

Fix the path from menu to payment before adding anything new to the website.
